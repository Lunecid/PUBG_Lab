\section{Feature Dictionary}
\label{app:features}

Tables~\ref{tab:feat-dict-a} and~\ref{tab:feat-dict-b} define all 66
team-phase features. Every quantity is computed from observations within the
cut phase (Subsection~\ref{sec:methods-features}). The scaling column gives
the fixed constant by which the raw value is divided; ``log'' denotes a
\(\log(1{+}x)\) transform, ``binary'' a 0/1 indicator, and ``map scale'' a
fixed map-extent constant shared by zone radii and distances.
