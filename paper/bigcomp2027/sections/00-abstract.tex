Battle royale games remove teams phase by phase as a safe zone shrinks, which suggests a discrete-time hazard over safe-zone phases. We ask where predictive information about next-phase elimination resides: in each team's phase-by-phase history, in the other teams alive in the same phase, in typed relations between teams (proximity, combat escalation, zone co-pressure), or in an explicit safe-zone object node. We switch these components on and off inside one relation-biased attention backbone with a fixed loss and training setup; the configuration with all of them on is called PTR-Former.
