\section{PTR-Former and the Arms}\label{sec:method}

PTR-Former reads, for every phase of a match, a graph of team nodes, one safe-zone object node, and typed relations. It applies relation-biased attention along the team axis and causal attention along the phase axis, and two heads read a next-phase hazard and a within-match standing score. Each component is a switch, and all arms share the backbone, the loss, and the training setup; we drop the match index $m$ below.

\emph{Graph.} Team nodes are the teams alive at the start of phase $k$, each with its feature vector $\mathbf{x}_{i,k}$ (150 features, the normalised 2-D centroid $\mathbf{c}_{i,k}$ at the phase boundary, and the team's AI share). The safe-zone object node $z_k$ carries raw zone geometry only, $\mathbf{u}_k$; processed quantities such as boundary distance are left out on purpose: RQ4 asks whether the model recovers them. The team--team relations are \texttt{prox} and \texttt{copress} (symmetric), \texttt{combat\_out} (attacker to victim), and \texttt{combat\_in} (its transpose), with the raw weights of Section~\ref{sec:repr} and no row normalisation. Combat replaces proximity for a pair that exchanged damage in the phase (escalation): $W_{\mathrm{prox}}=(1-E)\odot w^{\mathrm{prox}}$ with $E_{ij}=1$ if the pair exchanged damage, and combat biases are learned separately from the proximity bias. The team--object edge is a continuous bias of the centroid's position relative to the zone: $\mathbf{g}_{i,k}\in\R^6$ holds the offset and the distance to the current and the next zone, each divided by the zone radius, and a two-layer MLP ($6\to16\to H$, one per block) maps it to one bias $\beta_h(\mathbf{g}_{i,k})$ per head.

\emph{Attention.} Each block applies multi-head attention~\cite{vaswani2017attention} along the phase axis of each team (the zone token takes no part), then along the team axis within each phase over the team tokens and the zone token, then a feed-forward layer, with residual connections and layer normalisation; $L$ blocks are stacked. With the phase switch $s_P$, team token $(i,k)$ attends to phases $l\le k$ ($s_P=1$) or only to $l=k$ ($s_P=0$). With the team switch $s_T=0$ a team attends only to itself; otherwise it attends to the teams $j$ alive at the start of the phase and, if $s_Z=1$, to the zone token. The logit from team $i$ to team $j$ in head $h$ is
\begin{equation}
e^{(h)}_{ij,k}=\frac{\mathbf{q}^{(h)\top}_{i,k}\mathbf{k}^{(h)}_{j,k}}{\sqrt{d_h}}+s_R\sum_{r\in\mathcal{R}}b_{r,h}\,W_{r,k}[i,j],
\label{eq:team-logit}
\end{equation}
with $\mathcal{R}=\{\mathrm{prox},\mathrm{out},\mathrm{in},\mathrm{cop}\}$ and $b_{r,h}$ learned per head and block (zero diagonal; a team may attend to itself). The logit to the zone token adds $\beta_h(\mathbf{g}_{i,k})$ instead; the zone token is both a query and a key, updated in every block, with the same symmetric bias. The representation $\mathbf{z}_{i,k}$ therefore depends only on tokens of phases $\le k$ and respects $\mathcal{F}^m(k)$.

\emph{Heads and loss.} Both heads read $\mathbf{z}_{i,k}$: a hazard head $\hat\lambda_{i,k}=\sigma(\mathbf{w}_h^\top\mathbf{z}_{i,k}+c_h)$ and a rank head with a standing score $s_{i,k}$ (larger is a better expected placement). The loss is $\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{haz}}+0.1\,\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{rank}}$ in every arm, where $\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{haz}}$ is the binary cross-entropy over at-risk rows (the discrete-time survival likelihood) and $\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{rank}}$ averages $\log(1+e^{-(s_{i,k}-s_{j,k})})$ over team pairs in the risk set in which $i$ finished ahead of $j$.

\emph{Player layer (variant M11RZ-P).} Player nodes are the players alive at the start of the phase, each with its boundary-time position (relative to the team centroid and in map coordinates), health, damage dealt and taken, knocks and kills, cumulative kills and healing-item uses, and the team's features. Every player attends over all alive players, with relations as logit biases: teammate (same team, with distance features), enemy proximity (other teams, within 300 m, by distance bin), combat (replacing proximity), and object (player to zone). Player tokens are pooled to teams by attention pooling and then enter the phase axis and the hazard head. M11RZ-P$-$tm removes only the teammate bias.

\emph{Arms.} Switching the phase axis off restricts attention to the current phase, and switching the team axis off restricts each team to itself; M00, M10, M01, and M11 form a 2$\times$2 factorial design (Table~\ref{tab:arms}). M11R-shuf permutes the team indices of the relation matrices within match and phase and is retrained. The noHab arms and HGB-noHab remove the Hab block (45 of the 150 features; the two alive counts stay), and HGB-lag adds the previous phase's values and differences (missing at $k=1$). The recovery ratio is $\kappa=[\mathrm{AUC}(\text{M11RZ-noHab})-\mathrm{AUC}(\text{M11R-noHab})]/[\mathrm{AUC}(\text{M11R})-\mathrm{AUC}(\text{M11R-noHab})]$; its numerator is the confirmatory contrast P5.
