% Abstract of the final storyline (narrative brief, 2026-10-06).  Numbers: reports/bigcomp2027_results/.
Battle royale games eliminate teams phase by phase as a safe zone shrinks. We study next-phase elimination as a discrete-time hazard on 13,948 public PUBG matches in 12 map-by-perspective-by-team-size cohorts. An audit of event telemetry shows that snapshot-derived labels are wrong for 11.4\% of training rows, so we use event-based labels on a single clock. On our task and data, a phase-conditioned representation of zone and local-competition context scores 0.06--0.10 AUC above adapted implementations of earlier battle-royale approaches \todo{recheck the lower bound after the KRAFTON/K2 re-run}. A pre-specified decomposition on identical rows finds that phase history and explicit relation graphs add no meaningful predictive information (equivalent within a smallest effect of 0.005 AUC), that cross-team attention adds a small but consistent gain (+0.003 AUC, +0.009 Spearman), and that an explicit safe-zone object recovers about 29\% of the information in hand-crafted zone features.
